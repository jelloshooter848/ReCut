The films
---------

The four films in this kit are "open movies" made by the Blender Foundation and
released under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) licences, which allow anyone
to share and adapt them, also commercially, as long as the work is credited.

Every file in this kit that comes from a film is a MODIFIED copy: re-encoded (to
720p H.264 / AAC, or to the formats in formats/), cut into excerpts, rescaled,
cropped, given other frame rates, remixed (audio tracks and channels), or turned
into stills. The complete films in franchise/ keep their full credits. None of
this is endorsed by the Blender Foundation.

The subtitles
-------------

  The .srt files in franchise/ (Tears of Steel: English and German; Sintel:
  French) are the subtitles published with the films on download.blender.org,
  re-saved as UTF-8 text. They are shared with the films under the films'
  licences. Subtitles inside clips in formats/ are excerpts of the same files.
  Sintel has no official English subtitles; Big Buck Bunny has no dialogue;
  no official subtitles were found for Elephants Dream.

Everything else
---------------

Everything else in the kit was made for it, by a script, with FFmpeg: the
excerpts and their extra audio mixes, the picture subtitles of the "DVD rip"
(drawn from the official subtitle text), the tone, the noise and the silence,
the long timecode video, the title card, the deliberately broken files and the
messy subtitle file, the trailer captions and the two ReCut projects. Text in
pictures is drawn with the DejaVu Sans font. No third-party music or footage
is included.

Help > About ReCut > Licences for it and the programs it includes.
