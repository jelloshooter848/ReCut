# Media pipeline attack report

Scope: timestamps, A/V sync, frame mapping, codecs/streams, proxies and export graph of ReCut
(`shared/time.ts`, `shared/timeline.ts`, `electron/media/*`, `electron/export/renderGraph.ts`, `src/playback/*`).
Every number below was measured on real files produced by ffmpeg 6.1.1 (`tests/attack/gen-media.sh`) and run through
the real `probeMedia`, `runExport`, `startProxyJob`, `computeWaveform` and `getThumbnail` code. No mocks.

How the frames were checked: the counter sources burn the frame number into the picture as two grey levels
(top half = `16+4*(N mod 50)`, bottom half = `16+4*floor(N/50) mod 50`), so every exported frame can be decoded exactly
from a 2x2 area-scaled pixel read. The sync sources have a one-frame white flash and a 50 ms 1 kHz beep (`aevalsrc`,
sample-accurate) at every `t = 2k` s.

"Editor model" means what the Program Monitor shows. `SequencePlayer` seeks to `sourceTime + 0.5/mediaFps`
(`src/playback/sequencePlayer.ts:349`) and Chromium shows the frame that covers that time, so the editor shows media
frame `floor(sourceTime*mediaFps + 0.5)`. `tests/attack/e2e/chromium-seek.spec.ts` checks this in real Electron.

## Verdict

**Not frame-exact, and the preview is wrong for offset-start files; A/V sync is otherwise mostly sound.** Durations, frame counts and audio lengths come out exact at
23.976/24/25/29.97, and so does seek math. Audio placement is sample-accurate: 50 linked inserts put every beep within
0.00 frames of the model. The 300-clip graph builds in 10 ms and renders with 0 wrong frames. The picture does not
always match the editor:

- **Single-clip tracks drop or duplicate frames** at 23.976, 25 and 29.97. At 29.97, 27 % of the frames are wrong. The cause is float truncation in `setpts` (M-01).
- **MPEG-TS sources export 0.8–1 s early.** The picture is out of sync with its own audio by 1 s (M-02).
- **When sourceIn falls off the media frame grid, the export shows a different frame than the editor.** This happens after any trim of a clip whose rate differs from the sequence's. The export dropped 50 of 50 one-frame flashes that the editor shows (M-03).
- **Browser-playable files with a non-zero container start_time preview the wrong frames.** Real Chromium shows frame 0 where the export shows frame 100 (M-11).
- **Stream start offsets are lost for clips that start in the first second of a file** (M-04). The **proxy ignores the selected audio stream** (M-05, resolved in 0.4.0). **Display rotation is not in the probe** (M-06).

All fixes except M-05, M-06 and M-11 are a few lines in `renderGraph.ts`. Each one was validated with a patched graph:
`exportPatched` in `tests/attack/helpers.ts` swaps one filter and re-runs ffmpeg exactly like `exporter.ts`.

## What held up (measured, passing)

- **Seek math** (`seek.test.ts`): `frameCenterSeconds → secondsToFramesFloor` round-trips every frame of one hour at 23.976 (86 314 frames), 29.97 (107 893), 59.94 (215 785), 24 and 25, with 0 failures. This includes frames 0, 1, 1000, 86399 and 172000, µs rounding, and the `SequencePlayer.seek` +1e-6 epsilon.
- **Exact length**: 97-frame exports have exactly 97 frames at 23.976/24/25/29.97. A 23.976 export with AAC is 10.051708 s of video and 10.052 s of audio (within one AAC frame).
- **Container offsets on MP4/MKV**: `-output_ts_offset 10` MP4, B-frames with negative CTS (timescale 90000), and audio-late MP4 (edit list) and MKV clips taken mid-file: flash and beep agree within 1 frame.
- **Sync through speed, transitions and rate conversion**: 2x (flashes 12/36/60/84 vs beeps 11.7/35.7/59.6/83.8 frames), 0.5x, cross-dissolve + audio crossfade, dip-to-black + crossfade. A 25 fps source in 23.976/24/29.97 sequences stays within 1 frame for 5 markers each.
- **50 consecutive linked inserts** (shared `placeClips` insert at the head, 25 fps into 23.976): integer, contiguous positions; exported frames = model length (1597); every beep 0.00 frames from the model.
- **Streams and codecs**: absolute stream indexes are honoured (`[i:3]` gives 880 Hz on `subsfirst.mp4`; MKV `#2` 5.1 ac3 exports 6 channels). An MP4 whose first stream is audio exports the right picture (frame 24 at 1.0 s) and a 440 Hz tone. Mono → stereo gives equal L/R (−3 dB). 7.1 PCM MKV and 24-bit WAV export audibly at the exact length. 10-bit HEVC and H.264 4:2:2 export to yuv420p with exact frame counts and are flagged not playable with codec-specific reasons. MKV attachments and MP3 APIC cover art are skipped by the probe.
- **Proxies** (`proxy.test.ts`): 23.976 stays at 24000/1001, durations are 20 vs 20.01 s, counters are identical. ts10, TS and audio-late sources keep their flash/beep positions relative to the container start. An audio-only source gives an audio-only proxy. **The export never references a proxy path**, even when one is ready.
- **Graph edge cases** (`exportgraph.test.ts`): 300 clips; fades at the sequence edges; both-end transitions on a 10-frame clip (shortened with a warning, exact length); an inOut boundary inside a transition; V2 at 50 % over a V1 gap; 853x480 → aspect kept; rotated → portrait pillarbox; 4K upscale; bitrate mode and libx265; still images.
